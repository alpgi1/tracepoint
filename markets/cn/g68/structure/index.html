G68 China shows clear metric weaknesses in equipment perception, connected services, luggage loading, climate/NVH and braking, while brand prestige, reliability and core BMW driving character remain strengths; confidence is limited because there are no China customer comments or existing requirements in scope.

For G68 in China, the evidence is entirely quantitative plus two market-context rows: there are no customer comments, no subsystem-classified rows, no sales rows and no stored requirements. The CN_EU_2025 study points to the first priorities: equipment level is a major relative weakness at 7.49 and 2.33 below the peer mean, connected services is the second-weakest attribute at 7.35, ease of loading luggage is the weakest attribute at 7.25, braking is third-weakest at 7.38 and 0.44 below peer mean, climate control is seventh-weakest at 7.58 and 1.24 below peer mean, and quietness is sixth-weakest at 7.52 and 1.87 below peer mean. At the same time, roadholding 8.92, sportiness 8.86, prestige 8.90 and reliability 8.84 should be protected. In China market context, there is weak evidence that new-energy vehicles have overtaken combustion retail sales, and medium competitor evidence that in-car fridges are widely offered above 200,000 yuan, though actual usage is disputed. With no direct customer voice, recommendations should stay conservative and focus first on validating the equipment/content gap and digital-service gap, then on targeted H1 improvements where the current model can adapt.

What is open

Assumptions and gaps

  • There are no G68 China customer comments in the dataset, so no direct customer quotes can be provided and no complaint-theme drill-down was possible.
  • China has study scores but no customer comments in this scope; all customer insights therefore come from metrics and authored findings, not from raw customer language.
  • No stored requirements exist for G68 CN, so no recommendation could be linked to an existing REQ id.
  • No stored effort estimates exist for G68 CN; the change profiles above are analyst proposals based on the CPM reference points and must be treated as rough, assumption-based prioritisation only.
  • The subsystem view contains zero classified rows, so it cannot be used here to concentrate complaints in engineering terms.
  • Equipment searches for tested terms returned no matching catalogue items; the catalogue is the German July 2026 price list and may not reflect China standard/optional logic even when items exist on the model line.
  • Sales data for G68 CN is empty, so no volume weighting can be added.
  • BMW Group report passages on China and electrified demand are market context, not customer feedback; they should not be read as proof of a specific customer need on G68.
  • The competitor fridge row is medium evidence but contradictory: widespread offering is supported better than actual usage, so it can inform validation of the equipment gap but cannot justify a feature decision on its own.
  • Study scores are compared only with other BMW models, not competitors; a weak score means weak within BMW's portfolio evidence, not necessarily weak versus the external market.
