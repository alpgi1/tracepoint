Dual-zone climate as standard and rear ventilation

Proposal
1, implement. Climatisation (24 rows), climate control panel (24) and climate settings (17, nine wishes) describe synced temperatures that cannot be split and a display that looks dual-zone; the study scores climate control at 7.31 vs peer 8.13, the largest interior gap.
Horizon
H1. Software activation plus standard-content decision; rear vents in the life-cycle impulse.
Domain
Interior Comfort & Materials
Markets
EU
Evidence level
B (the best level of the primary and supporting findings)
Status
proposed, version 1
Reference
REQ-F70-EU-IC-02 (the key of the decisions, the audit trail and the exports)

Statement

Driver and passenger temperatures shall be adjustable independently on every variant, the display shall not suggest zones that are not fitted, and rear passengers shall get air vents.
