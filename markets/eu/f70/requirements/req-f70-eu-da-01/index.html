Assistance systems that help instead of irritating

Proposal
1, implement. Driver assistance draws 24 rows with 17 defects (unexpected braking) and the speed-limit assistant 16 rows (14 usability) because it must be switched off after every start.

Reference

Statement

Speed-limit warning and lane assistance shall be switchable off with one press per drive, and the emergency brake shall not intervene without a real obstacle.

One row per market where expectations differ. Basis: evidence is a measured study value or a count; assumption is the agent's proposal for the product manager to set.
