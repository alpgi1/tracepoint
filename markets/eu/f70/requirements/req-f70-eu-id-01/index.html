Physical controls for climate and a rotary controller option

Proposal
1, implement. Touch screen operation is the largest complaint theme of the F70 in Europe (141 rows, 7.4 % of all complaints, 91 usability); 25 owners explicitly ask for the iDrive controller back and the operating concept adds 30 rows. Many describe touch-only climate control as unsafe.
Horizon
H1. Physical climate keys and a controller are a life-cycle-impulse change of the centre console.
Domain
Infotainment & Digital Services
Markets
EU
Evidence level
B (the best level of the primary and supporting findings)
Status
proposed, version 1
Reference
REQ-F70-EU-ID-01 (the key of the decisions, the audit trail and the exports)

Statement

Temperature, fan and seat or steering wheel heating shall have physical controls, and a rotary controller (or an equivalent haptic control) shall operate map zoom, lists and shortcuts so the driver does not have to aim at the touch screen while driving.
