Comfortable ride on poor road surfaces

Proposal
1, implement. Suspension and damping draw 41 complaint rows (2.2 %, 12 wishes for a softer ride) and the study scores ride comfort at 9.20 vs peer 9.73; adaptive suspension is standard only on the M135.
Horizon
H1. Damper tuning and an adaptive-suspension option fit the life-cycle impulse.
Domain
Driving Experience & Dynamics
Markets
EU
Evidence level
B (the best level of the primary and supporting findings)
Status
proposed, version 1

Statement

Non-M variants shall offer a comfort-oriented suspension that absorbs sharp bumps on poor European roads without losing the car's sporty handling.
