Personal drive mode that is really personal

Proposal
1, implement. My Modes draws 36 complaint rows, 15 of them wishes: owners want to start in sport or efficient mode and to configure the personal mode instead of fixed presets.
Horizon
H1. Software.
Domain
Driving Experience & Dynamics
Markets
US
Evidence level
B (the best level of the primary and supporting findings)
Status
proposed, version 1

Statement

The personal mode shall let the driver combine steering, suspension, throttle, sound and energy settings freely, hide unused modes and start in the last chosen mode.
