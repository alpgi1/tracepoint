Front and rear seats comfortable for long drives across body sizes

Proposal
1, implement. Seats are the largest complaint theme of the interior (62 complaint rows, 2.4 % of all US complaints, level A) and both study attributes sit below the peer: front seat comfort 5.74 vs 6.04, rear seat comfort 5.27 vs 6.24. Customer comments name back pain, firmness and a lumbar support that cannot be flattened.
Horizon
H1. Cushion, contour and lumbar calibration change within the life-cycle impulse; no new architecture needed.
Domain
Interior Comfort & Materials
Markets
US
Evidence level
A (the best level of the primary and supporting findings)
Status
proposed, version 1
Reference
REQ-G60-US-IC-01 (the key of the decisions, the audit trail and the exports)

Statement

The front seats shall allow a pain-free two-hour drive for drivers from 1.60 m to 1.90 m, including a lumbar support that can be set completely flat, and the rear seat cushion and backrest shall match the comfort of the front seats.
