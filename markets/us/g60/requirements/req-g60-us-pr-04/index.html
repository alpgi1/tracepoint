Dependable public fast charging on US trips

Proposal
1, implement. Public charging draws 55 complaint rows (65 BEV mentions) and battery charging 31 more: broken stations, no NACS access, slow sessions and charge cut-offs make long trips stressful.
Horizon
H1. Adapter, network agreement and route-planner logic do not need a new vehicle generation.
Domain

B (the best level of the primary and supporting findings)

proposed, version 1
Reference
REQ-G60-US-PR-04 (the key of the decisions, the audit trail and the exports)

Statement

BEV owners shall be able to use the Tesla Supercharger (NACS) network without improvising, the route planner shall prefer stations that are reported working, and the car shall hold its advertised peak charging power for a meaningful part of the session.
