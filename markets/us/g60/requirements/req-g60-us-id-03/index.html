Driver profiles and settings survive updates and shared use

Proposal
1, implement. Driver profile complaints (25 rows) and software update complaints (15 rows) report seat memories, wake words and assistance settings wiped by updates and the wrong profile loaded.
Horizon
H1. Software.
Domain
Infotainment & Digital Services
Markets
US
Evidence level
B (the best level of the primary and supporting findings)
Status
proposed, version 1
Reference
REQ-G60-US-ID-03 (the key of the decisions, the audit trail and the exports)

Statement

Personal settings shall survive over-the-air updates; each profile shall support at least two keys or phones, and the car shall recognise the right driver when two key holders approach together.

Acceptance criteria

One row per market where expectations differ. Basis: evidence is a measured study value or a count; assumption is the agent's proposal for the product manager to set.
