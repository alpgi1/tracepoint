  2. REQ-G60-US-ID-01

Frequently used functions reachable with one touch or a physical control

Proposal
1, implement. The operating concept is a level-A recurring complaint (53 rows, 39 of them usability) and is linked to the study attribute infotainment menu structure (4.88). Touch screen (35 rows) and instrument cluster (38 rows) complaints repeat the same pattern: functions are buried in menus and pull the eyes off the road.
Horizon
H1. Mostly software (shortcut layer, widget placement); physical controls only where the panel allows them in the life-cycle impulse.
Domain
Infotainment & Digital Services
Markets
US
Evidence level
A (the best level of the primary and supporting findings)
Status
proposed, version 1

Statement

Climate temperature and fan, seat and steering wheel heating, drive mode, start-stop, assistance on/off, volume, map and phone shall each be operable with one touch or a physical control while driving, without leaving the current screen.
