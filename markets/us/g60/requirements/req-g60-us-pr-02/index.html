Reliable range prediction and cold-weather range guidance for BEV and PHEV

Proposal
1, implement. Electric range is the second-largest powertrain complaint (67 rows, 2.6 %, 83 of them BEV); customer comments report estimates that are "off" and range that collapses in winter.
Horizon
H1. Range estimation and guidance are software.
Domain
Powertrain, Range & Charging
Markets
US
Evidence level
B (the best level of the primary and supporting findings)
Status
proposed, version 1
Reference
REQ-G60-US-PR-02 (the key of the decisions, the audit trail and the exports)

Statement

The displayed remaining range shall stay within an agreed tolerance of the real range in cold weather, and the car shall explain the expected winter range and the effect of pre-conditioning before a trip.

Acceptance criteria

One row per market where expectations differ. Basis: evidence is a measured study value or a count; assumption is the agent's proposal for the product manager to set.
