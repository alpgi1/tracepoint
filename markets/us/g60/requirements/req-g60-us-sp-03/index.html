Keyless access on all four doors and easy door handling

Proposal
1, implement. Door open/close is level A (31 rows) and linked to the study attribute rear entry/exit (5.41 vs 6.0); comfort access (30 rows, 17 wishes) and lock/unlock (38 rows) complain that the rear doors lost the touch sensor of earlier generations.
Horizon
H1. Rear handle sensors and closing-force tuning fit the life-cycle impulse.

proposed, version 1
Reference

Statement

Comfort access shall lock and unlock from all four door handles, the doors shall close without slamming and the handles shall be easy to find in the dark.
