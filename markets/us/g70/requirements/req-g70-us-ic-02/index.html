Climate system that cools a large cabin fast and lets passengers direct the air

Proposal
1, implement. Vehicle climatisation (42 rows), blower (23) and air conditioning (20) are all level A; the study puts air conditioning performance at 4.48 vs peer 5.19 and climate control at 4.74 vs 5.14. Owners in hot states describe the car as undriveable for minutes after parking.
Horizon
H1. Vent design and blower calibration in the life-cycle impulse; controls in software.
Domain
Interior Comfort & Materials
Markets
US
Evidence level
A (the best level of the primary and supporting findings)
Status
proposed, version 1
Reference
REQ-G70-US-IC-02 (the key of the decisions, the audit trail and the exports)

Statement

The climate system shall cool the cabin quickly after sun soak, let front and rear occupants direct the airflow, and offer temperature and fan controls that do not require menus.
