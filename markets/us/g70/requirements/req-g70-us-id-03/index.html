Rear theatre screen usable without blocking the driver's rear view

Proposal
1, implement. Rear seat entertainment draws 34 complaint rows next to 20 likes: the lowered screen blocks the rear view and streaming often fails; 13 owners explicitly ask for a camera rear-view mirror, and rear visibility scores 5.23 vs 5.63.
Horizon
H1. Camera mirror and software fit the life-cycle impulse.
Domain
Infotainment & Digital Services

US

proposed, version 1
Reference
REQ-G70-US-ID-03 (the key of the decisions, the audit trail and the exports)

Statement

When the rear theatre screen or rear blind is deployed, the driver shall keep a full rear view (camera image in the interior mirror or on demand), and the screen shall be easy to operate and stream without cables.
