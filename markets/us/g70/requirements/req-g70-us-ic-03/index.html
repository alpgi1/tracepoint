Robust glass roof with an opening option and blinds that work

Proposal
1, implement. The panorama glass roof draws 38 complaint rows with 14 defects (cracks, bubbles) and 17 wishes for an opening roof; sun protection adds 27 rows (blinds stuck, operated only via the screen).

Statement

The panoramic glass roof shall not crack in normal use, shall limit heat entry, and an opening sunroof shall be offered; the rear and side blinds shall operate by button and not get stuck.
