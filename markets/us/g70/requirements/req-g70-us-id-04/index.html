Seamless smartphone integration and audio worthy of the flagship

Proposal
1, implement. Apple CarPlay (28 rows, level A) and pairing (16 rows) complaints match the study gaps in smartphone integration (4.79 vs 5.49) and phone pairing (5.03 vs 5.59); audio is level A (27 rows) with bass 4.97 vs 5.59 and clarity 5.06 vs 5.68.

Statement

Apple CarPlay and Bluetooth shall pair once, connect to the active driver's phone and play at the same loudness as native sources; the premium audio systems shall deliver the bass and clarity customers expect at this price.
