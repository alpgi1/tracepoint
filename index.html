Change Prediction Method

How Tracepoint turns a change on the vehicle into one effort figure.

  1. 12 subsystems

    The vehicle as subsystems, each with its share of a new vehicle generation's development effort.

  2. A change spreads

    It starts in one subsystem and spreads over the interfaces, at most 3 links deep; each link has a likelihood and an impact.

  3. One figure, one class

    What it reaches adds up: adapt below 15 %, major revision up to 50 %, a new generation beyond.
