## What customers say

**Equipment/content perception is the clearest relative weakness** (confidence medium)

In CN\_EU\_2025, 'Level of equipment' scores 7.49, rank 4 of 37 weakest attributes for G68 in China and 2.33 below the peer mean of other BMW models in the same market. This is the largest peer gap among the listed product attributes and points to a content or package issue before a pure engineering issue.

**Connected services are a top customer-perceived weakness** (confidence medium)

Connected services score 7.35, rank 2 of 37 weakest attributes in CN\_EU\_2025 for G68 China. By contrast, adjacent infotainment attributes are materially better: ease of use 8.35, features 8.37 and navigation 8.52. The issue therefore looks more like service ecosystem performance or relevance than a broad HMI usability problem.

**Practical loading is the weakest measured attribute despite decent luggage-capacity perception** (confidence medium)

Ease of loading/unloading luggage is the weakest attribute in the study at 7.25, while luggage capacity itself is much stronger at 8.73. Rear roominess is also strong at 8.71. This suggests the issue is not cabin or trunk volume in general, but access, opening geometry or loading convenience.

**Climate comfort and quietness are both weak enough to affect premium perception** (confidence medium)

Heating/ventilation/air conditioning scores 7.58, rank 7 of 37 weakest and 1.24 below the peer mean; quietness when driving scores 7.52, rank 6 of 37 weakest and 1.87 below peer mean. Build quality remains high at 8.81 and reliability at 8.84, so the premium gap appears to be more about comfort execution than basic quality trust.

**Braking is a specific dynamic weakness inside an otherwise strong driving package** (confidence medium)

Braking scores 7.38, rank 3 of 37 weakest and 0.44 below the peer mean, while roadholding is the strongest attribute at 8.92, sportiness is 8.86, ride comfort is 8.45 and manoeuvrability is 8.48. Customers appear to value the car's dynamic character overall, but braking pulls that package down.

**Brand and core BMW attributes are worth protecting** (confidence medium)

Prestige/manufacturer reputation scores 8.90, reliability 8.84, roadholding 8.92 and sportiness 8.86, all among the strongest attributes in CN\_EU\_2025. Overall satisfaction is still 8.77, but it trails the peer mean by 0.87, so the task is to close specific weaknesses without diluting these strengths.

**China market context raises the importance of electrified relevance and visible comfort features, but only as context** (confidence low)

There is weak market evidence that new-energy vehicle retail sales in China reached about 12.8 million and passed combustion cars for the first time. There is also medium competitor evidence that in-car fridges are standard in nearly every new-energy model above 200,000 yuan in China, but that row carries a contradiction because actual usage is disputed. These rows do not prove a G68 customer need on their own, but they help explain why equipment perception may be under pressure in China.
