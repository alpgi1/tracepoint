# BMW 5 Series: what to change, for whom, and when

**Prioritise H1 software and regional usability fixes now, keep major EV and China-market hardware shifts for the next generation, and protect the 5 Series’ dynamic strengths.**

Now, develop easier everyday operation across all markets through software-first shortcuts and better connected-service stability, because EU and US customers already struggle and China’s market evidence points the same way. Add US-only fixes for climate persistence, phone integration, start-stop memory, hands-free tailgate reliability and cabin storage, where the customer signal is clearly stronger and stored requirements already exist. Leave major EV-range improvement and any China-specific hardware or package changes for braking feel, front-space packaging and a more physical-control cockpit to the next generation, because the current evidence points to broader module changes or China has no verbatims. Keep the current ride, handling and brand strengths while making those changes.

Customer text: EU 111 verbatims · US 3,628 verbatims · CN no verbatims. Sales 2025: EU 115,000 · US 78,000 · CN 75,000.
