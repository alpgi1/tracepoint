# BMW 5 Series: what to change, for whom, and when

**Prioritise H1 improvements to everyday controls and climate use across markets, add regional fixes for US practicality and China cockpit expectations, keep the dynamic strengths, and leave any real electric-range gain to the next generation.**

The main product work now is easier everyday use: simpler access to frequent functions and more direct climate operation are supported by customer or study evidence in EU, US and CN, and they can be done on the current car. Regionally, the US needs practicality fixes such as storage and the hands-free tailgate, while China needs a stronger cockpit offer with more direct controls and better digital execution. EU also needs better range confidence now, but a meaningful electric-range increase is a successor topic. Keep the current ride, handling and drivetrain character, which is a broad strength across the regions.

Customer text: EU 111 verbatims · US 3,628 verbatims · CN no verbatims · JP 53 verbatims · KR 205 verbatims. Sales 2025: EU 115,000 · US 78,000 · CN 75,000.

## What each region expects

**EU** (Europe, 111 verbatims)
- Easy climate and infotainment operation without digging through screens; software/HMI complaints run at 41.4 per 100 verbatims (46 rows), connected services are 3rd weakest of 37, and climate control is 6th weakest of 37.
- Better electric range confidence and efficiency for electrified use; Electric Range complaints run at 13.5 per 100 (15 rows), fuel economy is 4th weakest of 37, and BEV share is 17.4% in 2025 rising to 21.7% in Jan-Aug 2026.
- More practical luggage and rear space for a large sedan; loading ease is 1st weakest of 37, rear roominess 5th weakest, and luggage capacity 7th weakest of 37.
- Watch body equipment quality and usability rather than launch a separate body change now; body equipment complaints run at 11.7 per 100 (13 rows), but the clearer near-term fixes sit in controls, loading and digital use.

**US** (United States, 3,628 verbatims)
- Frequently used functions reachable in one touch and simpler controls; controls usability is 11th weakest of 103 and operating-concept complaints run at 1.5 per 100 (53 rows).
- Climate control that is direct, strong in heat and keeps settings; climate control is 14th weakest of 103 and there is an existing H1 requirement for this.
- Better everyday storage and phone integration; interior storage is 15th weakest of 103 and there are existing H1 requirements for storage and phone integration.

**CN** (China, no verbatims)
- Connected services and smartphone integration that feel local and current; connected services are 2nd weakest of 37 and smartphone integration 10th weakest of 37.
- A cockpit with more direct controls despite a digital layout; level of equipment is 4th weakest of 37 and China-specific market rows show 25.3% criticise the lack of physical buttons.
- Watch braking and front-seat roominess on the current car, but do not open separate hardware work now; braking is 3rd weakest of 37 and front interior roominess 8th weakest of 37, yet no China verbatims are stored to show the exact failure mode.
- Strong AI assistant and advanced digital experience, but reliable enough to trust; 84% expect a proactive AI assistant while 56.5% use AI vehicle control less because of reliability concerns.

**JP** (Japan, 53 verbatims)
- Strong drivetrain and ride/handling character; likes are 26.4 per 100 for drivetrain and 26.4 for handling/riding.
- No clear regional weakness from the small sample; only 53 verbatims and no study for this region.
- Watch drivetrain complaints, which are 11.3 per 100 (6 rows), before changing the product.

**KR** (South Korea, 205 verbatims)
- Strong drivetrain and body-tech appeal; likes are 30.2 per 100 for drivetrain and 13.7 for body equipment/technology.
- Navigation and remote parking need watching, but the signal is regional and smaller than EU/US HMI issues; navigation complaints are 2.9 per 100 (6 rows), remote controlled parking 2.4 (5 rows).
- No study is stored for this region, so changes should wait for stronger confirmation unless shared with other markets.
