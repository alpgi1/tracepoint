## 1. What the team asked for

1. Vehicles from the brochures' technical-drawing pages, as dot clouds or dots and lines, that
   grow when clicked. The G70 brochure has no drawing, so the team chose to take it from the web
   and continue with F70 and G60.
2. Delete Antarctica from the map and make the map a bit larger.
3. Markets must not be empty: two or three figures per market on the map (units, verbatims,
   weakest domain), so the map is the first layer of the situation analysis.
4. Vehicle choice as cards; the vehicle asset used again on the next screen as the domain
   selector with a heat map on the body.

## 2. What was done

* **Vehicle drawings** (`frontend/docs/05-vehicle-drawings.md`, D-046). A pipeline
  (`scripts/vehicles/build.mjs`, sharp) reads the F70 and G60 brochure pages as vectors and the
  G70 BMW press specification sheet (fetched 2026-10-10, raster), strips the dimension
  annotations (glyphs, hairlines, small black arrowheads, straight-only paths, clip paths left
  intact; the G60 ground line and the G70 interior arrows erased by position), crops the side and
  top views, floods the silhouette and writes the lines as alpha-mask PNGs plus dot runs at two
  physical pitches (64 mm and 32 mm). Sources and licence notes are in
  `frontend/assets/vehicles/sources/README.md`. G68 shows the G60 body and says so.
* **Map** (D-044). Antarctica is off; the projection is fitted to a window of all other land with
  16 px padding; a selected market rests at 40 % of the viewport height; a flight to the current
  state settles at once. Scale gain over step 06 at 1440 by 900: 3.6 % from the window and 2.3 %
  from the padding; the fit is width-limited, so that is the available room without cropping
  Alaska or Russia.
* **Readouts** (D-045). Each market shows name, 2025 units, verbatims and the highest complaint
  share domain, or the lowest study score where no verbatims are classified (CN). All figures
  come from `frontend/content/markets.json`, generated by `scripts/build-content.py` from
  `reports/baseline/*.json`, `docs/sales_volumes.xlsx`, `docs/F70_G60_G68_G70_customer_studies.xlsx`
  and `config/`; every value carries its source in the file. Readouts hide while the map flies.
* **Market panel and cards** (D-047). The magnified market shows a panel with its figures and
  one card per vehicle (drawing, name, code, units, verbatims, weakest domain or study score);
  cards keep one width (420 px) whether a market sells two or three vehicles. A click grows the
  drawing into the stage geometry of the vehicle screen (480 ms, transform only) and navigates.
* **Vehicle screen** (D-048). `/markets/<market>/<vehicle>` for the seven pairs: the side and top
  views with zones per domain tinted by complaint share, hover and click on the body or in the
  domain list, the selected domain's top complaint themes with their breakdown (defects,
  difficult to use, wants); CN vehicles show the study's lowest scores and no heat map, with a
  plain statement why.

## 6. Open for the team

1. The body zones (`frontend/lib/vehicles/zones.ts`) are rectangles placed by the assistant;
   correct them, or decide on shapes that follow the body.
2. The G70 drawing is the 2022 press sheet (pre-facelift), softer than the vector drawings and
   with seats; a vector drawing from BMW would replace it by a file swap. Rights for the press
   material must be cleared before any public release.
3. The EU readout sits on the Atlantic beside Europe because Europe is too small at rest; say if
   a leader line or a different anchor is wanted.
4. The card-to-screen growth is followed by a route change (a cut at the end); a shared-element
   view transition would remove it.
5. The map is width-limited; further enlargement needs cropping or bleeding, both a team call.
